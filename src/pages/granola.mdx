## Ingredients

- Coconut oil
- Ground cardamom
- Ground cinnamon
- Honey or maple syrup
- Oats
- Nuts and seeds of your choice

## Method

1. Heat up the coconut oil in a pot.
2. Turn off the heat once it has liquified.
3. Add the ground cardamom and cinnamon.
4. Add the honey or maple syrup.
5. Add the oats and nuts/seeds of your choice to a bowl.
6. Pour the coconut oil over the ingredients in the bowl.
7. Cook for 45 mins at 160°C, stirring every 5-10 mins.
