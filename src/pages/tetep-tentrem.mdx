My head torch has gone out. Probably the first time I've actually used it and of course
I had forgotten to charge it. The guide kindly lends me his and continues his way up the
sheer rocky path, lighting his way with his phone. The mountains around us are dark silhouettes against
the sky, with little groups of lights from other hiking groups adorning the ridges. The valley below
continues to twinkle and the call to prayer has started. Every few minutes another tone joins the building cacophony of
voices, each one different, a crescendo building below us like a hive of bees.

The sun has started to make its presence known. I can see a line of pink across the ridges ahead of us and two
impossibly bright stars sit between our destination and the next ridge. As we reach the summit the valley below us starts
to come into focus and the lights that have been crawling up the hills around us in all directions slowly flicker out.

The sun is finally visible over the edge of a far off peak on another island, behind us the green
bulk of the volcano has finally come fully into view.
Coffee is brewing and the birds in the nearby woods start to wake up.
