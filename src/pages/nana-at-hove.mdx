The water is clear and blue, I can see my whole body when I look down, suspended by the salt.
The waves are high, I feel myself being lifted up and then pulled back down.

As I reach the top of a wave I look back to the shore and I see you sitting on the bench, where we left you.
It took you a long time to get here but you made it, holding onto our arms and stopping every
now and then to catch your breath. 

You're waving at us, I wasn't sure if you'd be able to see us from so far away but you can. From this distance
I can't tell that you struggle to walk now, or that you don't always know what is happening, to me it seems like 
you're watching over us, like you used to when we were young, protecting us whilst we swim.

"You're both very brave" you say as we dry off in the sun and you sit there quietly whilst we lie
down and close our eyes. I think you are the brave one really.
