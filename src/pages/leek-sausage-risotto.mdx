## Ingredients

---

- Leeks
- Mushrooms
- Sausages
- Risotto rice
- White or red cooking wine
- Soy sauce
- Salted butter (optional)
- Parmesan

## Method

---

1. Chop half the leeks and mushrooms.
2. Add them to a pan of boiling water with salt and a splash of soy sauce.
3. Boil for 10 minutes, then leave to simmer — this is your broth.
4. In another pan, fry the chopped sausage. Move it to a bowl once cooked.
5. Chop the rest of the leeks and mushrooms, fry them in the same pan, and add
   them to the bowl.
6. Add the risotto rice to the pan and fry briefly, then add a splash of wine.
7. Start ladling in the broth from the pot, vegetables included, one spoon at a
   time, adding more each time the liquid has boiled off.
8. Add a chunk of salted butter along the way if you want it creamier.
9. Once the rice has softened, stir the vegetables and sausage back in.
10. Add one last ladle of broth, grate in some parmesan and mix.
11. Dish into bowls.
