## Ingredients

- Salmon fillets
- Jasmine rice
- Avocado
- Cucumber
- Sriracha sauce
- Mayonnaise

### Marinade

- Soy sauce
- Fish sauce
- Honey
- Chilli flakes

## Method

1. Mix the marinade ingredients together.
2. Cover the salmon in the marinade and leave it.
3. Cook the jasmine rice as per the packet instructions.
4. Chop the avocado into long slices.
5. Chop the cucumber into small cubes.
6. Mix the mayonnaise with the sriracha.
7. Slowly pan fry the salmon.
8. Serve.
