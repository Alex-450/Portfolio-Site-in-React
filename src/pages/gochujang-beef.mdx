## Ingredients

---

- Minced beef
- Soy sauce
- Gochujang
- Red peppers
- Courgette (optional)
- Maple syrup (optional)
- Sesame seeds
- Jasmine rice

## Method

---

1. Brown the beef in a frying pan
2. Add red peppers and any other vegetables (e.g. courgette)
3. Add soy sauce
4. Add gochujang
5. Stir and let sauce coat the beef
6. Add a bit of maple syrup (can also use honey)
7. Take off the heat, add some sesame seeds
8. Serve with jasmine rice
