## Ingredients

- Beef steak
- Bimi broccoli
- Udon noodles
- Garlic
- Mushrooms (optional)
- Sesame seeds

### Sauce

- Sesame oil
- Corn starch
- Soy sauce
- Sugar
- Mirin

## Method

1. Mix all the sauce ingredients together.
2. Thinly slice the beef and mix with some of the sauce. Leave to marinade.
3. Fry the beef, then remove from the pan.
4. In the same pan, fry the garlic, broccoli and mushrooms (if using).
5. Once the veg is cooked, add the udon noodles and the rest of the sauce.
6. Add the meat back.
7. Sprinkle with sesame seeds.
8. Serve.
