## Ingredients

- Coconut milk
- Thai red curry paste
- Vegetables (mushrooms, courgette, red peppers etc.)
- Chicken or tofu
- Lime juice
- Lime leaves (optional)
- Soy sauce
- Fish sauce (optional)
- Jasmine rice

## Method

1. Heat the coconut milk.
2. Add the curry paste.
3. Add the vegetables.
4. Add the chicken or tofu — if using tofu, first dry, season and fry it.
5. Bring to the boil.
6. Leave to simmer.
7. Add the lime juice, lime leaves (if using), soy sauce and fish sauce.
8. Cook the jasmine rice as per the packet instructions.
9. Serve.
